# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-05

Marky is an offline-first bookmark manager. This policy describes how the app handles data for the current production release line.

## Data Marky stores locally

Marky may store the following on your device:

- Saved bookmark URLs and metadata
- Titles, descriptions, thumbnails, favicons, and scraped page metadata
- Tags, collections, notes, archive/read/favorite state, and reminders
- Automation rules and automation debug history
- App settings such as theme, language, and reminder preferences
- Local backup/export files when you choose to create them

## Network access

Marky uses internet access to fetch metadata for links you save, such as page titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and favicons. The saved URL is requested from the destination website for this purpose.

## Camera access

Camera access is used for QR code scanning only. Marky does not use camera access for OCR, photo extraction, or background capture.

## Notifications and reminders

Marky can schedule local notifications for bookmark reminders and unread reminders. Notification content is generated locally from your saved data.

## Data sharing

Marky does not sell personal data. The current app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

When you open or save external links, the destination website may receive normal web request information such as your IP address and device/network metadata.

## Backups and restore

Backup files are created only when you choose to export data. You are responsible for where those files are stored or shared.

## Deleting data

You can delete saved content inside the app. You can also remove all local app data using Android system settings by clearing Marky storage or uninstalling the app.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app maintainer through the distribution channel where you received Marky.
